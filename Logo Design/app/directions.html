Kimi Nola · Identity directions · v1 · open source

Four marks for a voice
that takes notes.

Kimi Nola is two tools in one — meeting notes (the Granola side of the name) and voice dictation (the Wispr Flow side). Each direction below is a hand-crafted SVG: no raster, no font dependencies in the marks, ready to drop into a Tauri icon pipeline. The 16 px column is rendered live, not simulated — what you see is what ships.

01 Oatwave the grain that listens
kimi nola

An oat grain — the granola namesake — whose natural striations are a live waveform. The husk is the clip mask, so some bars end flush at the shell like real grain texture. Warm, organic, editorial.

02 N-Bars an n you can hear
KIMI NOLA

Five equalizer bars whose tops trace the shoulder of a lowercase n — the letterform is the waveform. The valley bar (the dip of the n) is the one light note. Loud, geometric, survives 16px.

Kimi Nola

NOLA is the Crescent City — three concentric C-arcs open to the right like broadcast rings, and the floating dot is the moon, the mic, the note head. Poster-duotone attitude for the open-source crowd.
