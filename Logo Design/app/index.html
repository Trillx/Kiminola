  • One gold element per view. Gold is emphasis — if everything is gold, nothing is.
  • No gradients, no new hues. Semantic states derive from this ladder, never fresh colors.
  • Hairlines are rgba(36,36,36,.14) on light, rgba(242,239,233,.09–.10) on dark. Never heavier.
