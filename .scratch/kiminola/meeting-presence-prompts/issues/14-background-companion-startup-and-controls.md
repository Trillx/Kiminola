# Background companion startup and controls

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Claimed by: Codex
Blocked by: Background companion lifecycle

## Question

When should the Background companion become active? Decide whether it is opt-in during onboarding, whether it starts with Windows, how users pause or disable detection, and which tray/menu controls make its resident state visible without making the app noisy.

## Resolution

Background detection is explicit opt-in. The user may enable **Start with Windows** and control the companion from the tray with **Pause detection**, **Open Kimi Nola**, and **Quit**. It is not silently enabled by merely installing or closing the app.

The exact active/paused status indicator remains open for a follow-on ticket.
