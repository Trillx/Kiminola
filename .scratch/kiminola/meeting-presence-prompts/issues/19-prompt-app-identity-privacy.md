# Prompt app-identity privacy

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Claimed by: Codex
Blocked by: Initial app coverage and unknown apps

## Question

What app identity may the Meeting prompt display? Decide whether known families may use friendly names such as “Granola” or “Teams,” while unknown signals use generic wording and never expose executable paths, window titles, browser URLs, calendar titles, or provider metadata.

## Resolution

Known supported families may display only a friendly label such as **Granola** or **Teams**. Unknown detections use generic wording such as **possible meeting**. Kimi Nola must not expose or persist executable paths, raw window titles, browser URLs, calendar titles, or provider metadata merely to explain a prompt.
